Your girlfriend asks you the most dangerous question in the world. You're on the couch. A movie is on. A pretty actress shows up. She stops the movie. "Do you think she's pretty?" You think of every answer. "Yes" is wrong. "No" is a lie, and she knows it. You say: "She's okay." Wrong. "Just okay? So you looked at her." You didn't look. You were watching a movie. That's what a movie is for.
