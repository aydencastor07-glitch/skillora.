## 1) Settings → Policies → Shipping policy
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Shipping Policy

We offer FREE standard shipping on all orders to the United States.

Processing time: orders are processed within 1–3 business days.
Delivery time: 3–8 business days after processing.

As soon as your order ships, you'll receive an email with your tracking number.
Delays can occasionally happen during holidays or because of the carrier. If your package hasn't arrived within the estimated time, contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we'll make it right.

We currently ship to the United States only.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
## 2) Settings → Policies → Refund policy
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Refund Policy

Your satisfaction comes first. If something isn't right, we're here to help.

30-day guarantee: you can request a return within 30 days of receiving your order. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

Damaged or wrong item: if your item arrives damaged, defective, or isn't what you ordered, email us at [SUPPORT EMAIL] within 14 days with your order number and a photo. We'll send a free replacement or a full refund — no need to return the item.

Package not received: if your tracking shows no movement for 10 business days, or your order hasn't arrived 30 days after purchase, contact us and we'll reship it or refund you in full.

How to request a return: email [SUPPORT EMAIL] with your order number. We'll reply within 24–48 hours with the next steps. Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days after approval.

You'll receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.

Yes — within 30 days of delivery, if the item is unused and in its original packaging.
