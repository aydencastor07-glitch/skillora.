Every eye color, explained. Blue eyes. Here's the weird part: blue eyes contain zero blue pigment. None. Brown eyes are packed with melanin, a dark pigment. Blue eyes have almost none. So where does the blue come from? Light. When light enters your iris, tiny fibers scatter the blue part back out – the same reason the sky looks blue. And it gets stranger. Scientists believe every blue-eyed person on Earth shares one single ancestor. One tiny mutation, six to ten thousand years ago, copied into hundreds of millions of people. So… what color are your eyes?
