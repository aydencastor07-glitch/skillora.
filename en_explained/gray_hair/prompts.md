## ÉTAPE 2 – LES VIDÉOS (5), image → vidéo, 5 secondes chacune

### VIDÉO 1 = IMAGE 3 + ce prompt (le miroir, premier cheveu blanc)
```
The character leans closer to the mirror, spots the single white hair, eyes go wide in surprise and it slowly raises one hand to touch the white hair. Subtle, smooth movement, camera slowly pushes in. Keep the same cartoon style and white background.
```

### VIDÉO 2 = IMAGE 5 + ce prompt (l'usine de couleur dans la racine)
```
The tiny worker characters walk in a line and pour their brown paint buckets into the hair, the hair shaft slowly grows upward and fills with brown color. Gentle looping movement, cheerful. Keep the same cartoon style and white background.
```

### VIDÉO 3 = IMAGE 9 + ce prompt (les cellules souches coincées)
```
The character pushes and wiggles but stays stuck in the doorway, sweat drops fly off, then it slumps with a tired face. Funny, smooth animation. Keep the same cartoon style and white background.
```

### VIDÉO 4 = IMAGE 13 + ce prompt (le stress qui blanchit)
```
Small lightning bolts flash from the cloud and the character's hair slowly turns from brown to white strand by strand while it panics. Smooth, slightly comedic. Keep the same cartoon style and white background.
```

### VIDÉO 5 = IMAGE 18 + ce prompt (le personnage qui parle (fin))
```
The character looks straight at the camera and talks in a friendly way, mouth moving naturally, small hand gestures, then points at the viewer at the end. Keep the same cartoon style and white background.
```
Si ton outil fait parler le personnage (lip-sync), utilise la dernière phrase de la voix : « So when did you find your first gray hair? »
