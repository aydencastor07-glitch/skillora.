PARTIE 1 (473 caractères)
Every hair on your head, explained. Gray hair. You were born with color in every single hair. Then one morning, in the mirror, you find it. Your first gray hair. Here's what's actually happening. At the root of each hair, there's a tiny pigment factory. Its workers are called melanocytes, and they inject color into your hair while it grows. Every time a hair falls out and grows back, a team of stem cells sends in fresh workers. But with age, those stem cells get stuck.

PARTIE 2 (377 caractères)
Scientists discovered in 2023 that they stop moving to where they're needed. No workers, no color. The hair grows back completely white. And here's the thing: gray hair doesn't really exist. It's white hairs mixed in with your colored ones. So what about stress? In mice, scientists showed that a stress signal from the nerves burns through those stem cells in just a few days.

PARTIE 3 (228 caractères)
But here's the twist: in a 2021 study, some gray hairs got their color back when people's stress went down. And no, pulling out a gray hair won't make two grow back. One root, one hair. So when did you find your first gray hair?
