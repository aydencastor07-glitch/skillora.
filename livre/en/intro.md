# Introduction

You know these moments. You say something you regret the second it leaves your mouth. You let one message ruin your whole day. You know exactly what you should do – and you still don't do it.

It's not because you're weak. It's because nobody ever taught you the rules.

The greatest thinkers in history – Seneca, Marcus Aurelius, Epictetus, Nietzsche, Machiavelli – found exactly these rules. They faced the same problems you do: anger, fear, fake friends, wasted time. But their answers are scattered across old books that almost nobody finishes.

This book keeps only what works. **21 laws. Each one on a single page.** No long theories – just the law, what a great thinker said about it, why it's true, and what you can actually do today.

## How to use this book

**One law a day.** Read one law in the morning. Do the exercise that same day. Nothing more.

**Don't just read – apply.** A law you only know changes nothing. A law you've applied once stays with you.

**Check it off.** Under each law you'll find a small box. After 21 days, you'll see in black and white what you've done.

Three weeks. 21 laws. A different mind.

Let's begin.
