You've done hundreds of curls and your arms still look the same? It's not your genetics — it's the exercises.

Your triceps is about two thirds of your upper arm, and muscles grow more when you train them in a stretched position. In this video: the 2 exercises that actually build bigger arms (with a no-equipment version for each), a bonus muscle almost nobody trains, the 3 mistakes that keep your arms small, and a simple plan.

📋 The plan (2–3x per week)
• Overhead triceps extension – 3 sets of 8–12
• Incline dumbbell curl (or chin-ups) – 3 sets of 8–12
• Hammer curls – 2–3 sets of 8–12
• 3 seconds down on every rep, add 1 rep or a little weight every week
