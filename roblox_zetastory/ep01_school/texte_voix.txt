In my school, your grades decide your height.
Get an A, you grow.
Get an F, you shrink.

I just failed my third test in a row.
Now I'm the size of a shoe.

The straight-A students are as tall as buildings.
They step over us like we're ants.

But nobody talks about this:
Every month, the tallest student gets called to the principal's office… and disappears.
The teachers say he graduated early.
Nobody asks questions.

Last night, I got so small I could slip under the principal's door.
So I did.

Inside, it was dark.
Hundreds of glass jars on his shelf.
And inside every jar… something was moving.

I climbed closer.
It was them.
The missing students.
The smartest kids in school — now smaller than my finger.

One of them hit the glass.
He screamed something.
I couldn't hear him.
So I read his lips.

He said: RUN.

That's when I understood.
Our principal is the tallest man I've ever seen.
Because he doesn't teach us.
He grows us.

Then the lights turned on.
And a giant voice said:
"Strange. I don't remember shrinking you yet."
