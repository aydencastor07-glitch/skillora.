In this school, your grades decide your height. Get an A, you grow. Get an F, you shrink. And I just failed my third test in a row. Now I'm the size of a shoe. The straight-A students are as tall as the building. They step over us like we're ants. But there's one thing nobody talks about. Every month, the tallest student gets called to the principal's office. And never comes back. The teachers say they graduated early. Nobody asks questions. Last night, I became so small that I could slip under the principal's door. So I did. Inside, it was dark. On his shelf, there were hundreds of glass jars. And inside every jar, something was moving. I climbed closer. It was them. The missing students. The smartest kids in school, now smaller than my finger. One of them hit the glass and screamed something. I couldn't hear it. So I read his lips. He said: run. That's when I understood why our principal is the tallest man I've ever seen. He doesn't teach us. He grows us. Then the lights turned on. And a giant voice said: strange. I don't remember shrinking you yet.
