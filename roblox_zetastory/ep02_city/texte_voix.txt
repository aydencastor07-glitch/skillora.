In my city, everyone has a number floating above their head.
It shows how many days you have left.

My mom has twelve thousand.
My best friend has nine thousand.
I have three.

People stare at me on the street.
The bus driver lets me ride for free.
My teacher hugged me today and started crying.

But this morning, something strange happened.
I walked past an old man in the park…
and my number went up.
Four.

I stepped closer.
Five.
Six.
And his number went down.

He looked at me and smiled.
"Take as many as you need," he said.
"That's how I got mine."

I looked above his head.
Seventy-eight thousand days.
More than two hundred years.

Then I noticed something.
Every old man on the benches had the same smile.
And they were all looking at me.

One of them stood up.
Then another.
My number started dropping.
Two.
One.

And somewhere behind me, a little girl screamed:
"Mommy… why is his number going UP?"
