In my family, we take one photo every year on my birthday.
And every year, there's one extra person in it.

Nobody remembers who it is.
Nobody remembers taking the picture with them.

On my fifth birthday, he was standing far away, behind the trees.
On my tenth, he was at the end of the garden.
On my fifteenth, he was right behind my dad.
Smiling.

My parents say it's a glitch in the camera.
They threw the camera away.
The next year, he was still there.

Tonight is my sixteenth birthday.
Mom said: "Everyone, get closer!"
The flash went off.

I looked at the photo.
Mom. Dad. My little sister.
And him.
Standing exactly where I was standing.
Wearing my clothes.

I wasn't in the picture.

I looked up.
My family was hugging him.
My mom was crying: "Happy birthday, sweetheart."

I screamed: "Mom, that's not me!"
She turned around.
She looked right at me…
and said: "Sorry… do we know you?"
