In my town, on your thirteenth birthday, you get two buttons.
Red: you get one million dollars.
Blue: your best friend gets ten million.
And nobody ever finds out what you pressed.

Last week was my best friend's birthday.
He came out of the room… and he wouldn't look at me.
Two days later, his family bought a new house.

Today it's my turn.
Two buttons.
And a little screen that says:
"Your friend pressed red."

That's not supposed to happen.

If I press blue, the guy who betrayed me gets ten million.
If I press red… I become just like him.

So what do I press?
