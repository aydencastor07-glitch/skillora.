In my town, when someone lies, their nose turns bright red.
So nobody lies. Ever.

Today, my best friend came to school with a brand new bike.
The teacher asked: "Where did you get that?"
He said: "My dad bought it for me."
His nose stayed normal.

But his dad left two years ago.

So after school, I followed him.
He stopped at an old house at the end of my street.
A man opened the door.
My friend said: "Hi, Dad."
Still normal.

The man hugged him and said: "I missed you, son."
And his nose… turned bright red.

So who is he?
