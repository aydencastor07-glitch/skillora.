In my town, nobody goes outside during the day.
Because when the sun comes up, they come out.
Giant creatures, taller than buildings.
Every day, they take someone. And nobody ever comes back.
So we live at night. School at midnight. Groceries at 3 AM.

Yesterday, my little brother ran outside at noon.
I ran after him.
A giant hand grabbed us both.
I closed my eyes and waited.

But it carried us underground, into a huge cave.
Full of people. All the missing people. Alive.
An old man smiled and said: "They're not taking us. They're saving us."
Then he pointed at the sky.
The sun wasn't yellow anymore. It was red.

Now, every morning, the giants stand over our town and block the sun.
And for the first time in years… we play outside.
