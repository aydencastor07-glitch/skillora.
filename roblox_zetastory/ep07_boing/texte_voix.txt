In my town, everybody copies me.
It started when I won the basketball final. I jumped and shouted: BOING!
The next day, the baker jumped. BOING!
My teacher, in the middle of the test. BOING!
The bride, at her own wedding. BOING!
Even the dogs. BOING!
All night long. BOING! BOING! BOING!
Nobody could sleep.
So the mayor called a town meeting: "From today, BOING is forbidden!"
Silence. For the first time in weeks.
That night, I walked past the mayor's house.
Through the window, I saw him… alone… jumping on his bed.
…boing.
