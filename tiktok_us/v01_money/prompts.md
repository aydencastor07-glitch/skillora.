# US v01 – Money printer : 3 VIDÉOS IA courtes, style bonhomme bâton (aucune vraie personne)

Style commun (à la fin de chaque prompt) : dessin animé 2D, bonhommes bâton sur papier beige, comme les dessins de nos vidéos.

## VIDÉO 1 – l'accroche : la machine à billets chez soi (≈ 5 s)
```
2D hand-drawn cartoon animation: a simple stick figure character with a round head, dot eyes and thin black line arms and legs sits next to a big boxy cartoon money printing machine with two spinning rollers; the machine starts rattling and shoots out green cartoon banknotes one after another, they fly up and rain down around the stick figure, who jumps up, throws its arms in the air and laughs. Style: clean bold black outlines, flat soft colors, warm beige paper background with subtle paper texture, smooth simple animation, static camera with a slight push-in. Cartoon banknotes are plain green rectangles with a simple circle, no numbers, no portraits, no real currency. No text, no letters, no subtitles, no logos, no watermark, no dialogue, no music. Vertical 9:16, 5 seconds.
```

## VIDÉO 2 – 1923 : la brouette pleine de billets (≈ 5 s)
```
2D hand-drawn cartoon animation in a faded sepia old-photo color palette: a stick figure with a flat cap and a little 1920s coat pushes a wooden wheelbarrow piled high with bundles of paper money along a simple cobblestone street, the pile wobbles and a few bundles fall off behind it, the stick figure stops, looks back and sighs. Simple cartoon buildings in the background. Style: clean bold black outlines, flat soft sepia colors, beige paper background texture, smooth simple animation, slow sideways camera pan following the stick figure. Cartoon money with no numbers and no portraits. No text, no letters, no subtitles, no logos, no watermark, no dialogue, no music. Vertical 9:16, 5 seconds.
```

## VIDÉO 3 – 1923 : on chauffe le poêle avec des billets (≈ 5 s)
```
2D hand-drawn cartoon animation in a faded sepia old-photo color palette: inside a tiny plain kitchen, a stick figure with a little apron kneels in front of an old black cast-iron stove and feeds bundles of paper money into it; the bills catch fire with cozy orange cartoon flames, the stick figure warms its hands and shrugs; next to the stove a single small log of wood sits ignored with a price tag. Style: clean bold black outlines, flat soft sepia colors with bright orange flames, beige paper background texture, smooth simple animation, static camera. Cartoon money with no numbers and no portraits. No text, no letters, no subtitles, no logos, no watermark, no dialogue, no music. Vertical 9:16, 5 seconds.
```
