Imagine you had a machine at home that prints money. New phone? Just print a thousand dollars. But that's illegal: counterfeiting can get you up to 20 years in prison. The government, though, is allowed to print money. So why not print enough to make everyone rich? Germany tried it. In 1923, the presses ran day and night. By November, one loaf of bread cost 200 billion marks. People carried their cash in wheelbarrows and burned it to heat their homes. Because money is just a ticket for real things. Double the money, not the bread, and the price doubles. If everyone is rich, nobody is. Would you still want one?
