=== PARTIE 1 (354 caractères) ===
Imagine never paying for gas again. You fill your tank with water, and your car starts. In the 1980s, an American named Stanley Meyer claimed he had done it: Los Angeles to New York on 22 gallons of water. Then, in 1998, at dinner with investors, he grabbed his throat, ran outside and collapsed. His brother said his last words were: "They poisoned me."

=== PARTIE 2 (260 caractères) ===
Millions still believe oil companies silenced him. But the coroner found a brain aneurysm. And a court had already called his invention fraud. Because water is hydrogen that has already burned. Your car would stop after a few feet. Would you have believed him?
