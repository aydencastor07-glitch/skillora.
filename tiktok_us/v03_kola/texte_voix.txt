Imagine drilling a hole straight through the Earth. How deep do you think you'd get? In 1970, the Soviet Union tried exactly that. In the far north of Russia, they started drilling. The hole was only as wide as a dinner plate. They drilled for almost 20 years. And in 1989, they reached 40,230 feet. That's 7.6 miles. Deeper than the deepest point of the ocean. The deepest hole humans have ever drilled. Then a story spread: scientists had lowered a microphone into the hole … and heard screams. Screams from hell. Millions of people still believe it today. But the truth is: the recording was fake. The screams came from an old horror movie. Their real goal was about 9 miles. The real problem was much simpler: the heat. Scientists expected around 212 degrees Fahrenheit down there. They measured 356. The rock got so hot, it started acting like play dough. Every time they pulled the drill out, the hole slowly flowed shut again. In 1992, they gave up. Today, all that's left is a rusty metal cap. And the craziest part? Those 7.6 miles are only 0.2 percent of the way to the center of the Earth. There are still almost 4,000 miles to go. They didn't even make it through the crust.
