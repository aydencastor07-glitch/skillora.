Law No. 1: Never reveal all your intentions. When you tell everyone what you're planning, you give them the tools to predict you… and sometimes, to stop you. Total honesty isn't always strength. Sometimes, it's a weakness. Machiavelli said: "Everyone sees what you appear to be. Few really know what you are." So stop explaining yourself. Let your actions speak. Become unpredictable.
