# Law No. 1 – Never suffer twice (voix clonée, script brut)

Même texte que law01_v2, sans les tags d'émotion ni les « … » de pause. Une seule partie.

Law number one. Never suffer twice. Most of your pain has not happened yet. You replay the argument. You imagine the failure. You lose the battle in your head before it even begins. Seneca said: "We suffer more often in imagination than in reality." So stop feeding the fear. Face what is real. Ignore what is not. The man who controls his mind cannot be controlled.
