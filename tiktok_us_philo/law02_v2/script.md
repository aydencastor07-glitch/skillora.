Law number two. Never show your anger. When you explode in front of everyone, you show them exactly which button controls you, and sometimes, how to destroy you. Anger feels like strength. In reality, it's losing control. Seneca said: "The greatest remedy for anger is delay." So breathe. Wait. Only answer once you're calm. Imagine nobody can provoke you anymore: calm, in control, respected. Follow for law number three.
