Law number two. Never let them see you angry. The moment you explode, you hand them the remote control to your mind. They push one button, and you react. Every single time. Anger feels like power. It's the opposite. Epictetus said: "Remember that it is not he who reviles or strikes you who insults you, but your opinion that these things are insulting." So when they push, stay still. Say nothing. Let them wonder. The calmest person in the room always holds the power. Follow for law number three.
