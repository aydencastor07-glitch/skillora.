# Law No. 3 – Talk less. (Zénon de Citium)
Voix clonée : chaque script en UNE seule partie, sans tags ni pauses.
Citation : Zénon de Citium, rapportée par Diogène Laërce (Vies des philosophes, VII, 23).

## TikTok
Law number three. Talk less than you think you should. Every time you over-explain, you hand people a map of your weaknesses. The more you say, the more they can use against you. Silence makes people uncomfortable, and uncomfortable people reveal themselves. Zeno said: "We have two ears and one mouth, so that we may listen more and talk less." So next time, answer in one sentence. Then stop. Let them fill the silence. Follow for law number four.

## Instagram / Facebook (quelques détails différents)
Law number three. Say less than necessary. When you explain too much, you are not being honest, you are being predictable. Every extra word is information someone can use. The quiet person in the room is the one everyone tries to read. Zeno said: "We have two ears and one mouth, so that we may listen more and talk less." So today, listen twice as long as you speak. Then watch what people tell you. Save this, and follow for law number four.
