Law number three. Say less than necessary. When you explain too much, you are not being honest, you are being predictable. Every extra word is information someone can use. The quiet person in the room is the one everyone tries to read. Zeno said: "We have two ears and one mouth, so that we may listen more and talk less." So today, listen twice as long as you speak. Then watch what people tell you. Save this, and follow for law number four.
