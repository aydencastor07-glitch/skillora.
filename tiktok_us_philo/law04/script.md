# Law No. 4 – Never take revenge. (Marc Aurèle)
Voix clonée : chaque script en UNE seule partie, sans tags ni pauses.
Citation : Marc Aurèle, Pensées (Meditations) VI, 6 — traduction anglaise de George Long (domaine public).

## TikTok
Law number four. Never take revenge. The moment you strike back, you show them exactly where it hurts. Revenge keeps you tied to the person who hurt you, for weeks, sometimes for years. And they win twice: once when they hurt you, and again when they turn you into someone you are not. Marcus Aurelius said: "The best way of avenging thyself is not to become like the wrongdoer." So don't answer. Don't explain. Become better, and let them watch. Follow for law number five.
