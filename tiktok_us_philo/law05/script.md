## Facebook / Instagram (version demandée en premier)
Law number five. Let them underestimate you. When people think you are weak, they stop watching you. They talk too much, they relax, and they show you every move before they make it. Sun Tzu said: "When able to attack, we must seem unable; when using our forces, we must seem inactive." So stay quiet. Let them laugh. Keep working while they sleep. Save this, and follow for law number six.

## TikTok (quelques détails différents, pour plus tard)
Law number five. Never let them see how strong you really are. The moment people know your power, they prepare for it. But when they think you are nothing, they get careless, and careless people make mistakes. Sun Tzu said: "When able to attack, we must seem unable; when using our forces, we must seem inactive." So say less. Show less. Let your results surprise them. Follow for law number six.
