## Facebook / Instagram (version demandée en premier)
Law number six. Choose your circle carefully. You slowly become the people you spend your time with. Their habits become your habits. Their excuses become your excuses. Seneca said: "Associate with those who will make a better man of you." So look at the five people closest to you. If they keep pulling you down, step back. Save this, and follow for law number seven.

## TikTok (quelques détails différents, pour plus tard)
Law number six. Protect who gets close to you. One wrong friend can cost you years. They laugh at your goals, they waste your time, and they call it loyalty.
