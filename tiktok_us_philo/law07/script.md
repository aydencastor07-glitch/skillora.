## Instagram / Facebook
Law number seven. Master yourself first. If you can't control your phone, your anger, or your appetite, you will never control your life. Every time you give in, something else decides for you. Epictetus said: "No man is free who is not master of himself." So start small. Wake up when you said you would. Finish what you start. Save this, and follow for law number eight.
