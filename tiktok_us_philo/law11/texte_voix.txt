Law number eleven. Find your why. Most people don't quit because it's too hard. They quit because they forgot why they started. Nietzsche said: "He who has a why to live can bear almost any how." So write your reason down. Read it every morning. And when you want to quit, read it again. Imagine never giving up on yourself again. That's exactly what the 21 laws in this book give you.
