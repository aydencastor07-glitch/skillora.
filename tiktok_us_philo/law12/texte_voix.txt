Law number twelve. Stop giving your time away. You would never hand a stranger your money. But every day, you give your time to people and things that don't deserve it. Seneca said: "It is not that we have a short time to live, but that we waste a lot of it." So today, notice where your hours really go. Cut one thing that steals them.
