Law number fourteen. Know yourself. Most people know everything about others. What they like, what they did, what they should do. But they never look at themselves. Socrates said: "The unexamined life is not worth living." So tonight, ask yourself one question: why did I do what I did today? Write the answer down. Do it every day for one week.
