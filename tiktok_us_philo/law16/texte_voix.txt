Law number sixteen. Become what you repeat. You keep saying you'll change tomorrow. Then today, you do the exact same thing again. Aristotle said: "We become just by doing just acts, temperate by doing temperate acts, brave by doing brave acts." You don't become strong by thinking about it. You become strong by doing it, again and again. So pick one small act and repeat it every day this week. That's exactly what the 21 laws in this book give you. The link is below.
